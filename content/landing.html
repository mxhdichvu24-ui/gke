About GKE Logistics

GKE Logistics International Co., Ltd.
 GKE specializes in providing cross-border logistics solutions for businesses, manufacturers, fulfillment centers, and e-commerce sellers.
 With over 20 years of experience in China, we have built strong capabilities in global shipping and large-scale supply chain management, with a focus on efficient shipping routes from Vietnam to the US and the UK.
 By combining global expertise with local execution, we deliver flexible and reliable logistics solutions tailored to e-commerce sellers.

Vision

To become a leading logistics partner from Vietnam to the world, setting new benchmarks for reliability, transparency, and performance in cross-border e-commerce logistics.

Mission

To deliver efficient, transparent, and scalable logistics solutions, enabling our clients to expand globally while maintaining operational excellence.
